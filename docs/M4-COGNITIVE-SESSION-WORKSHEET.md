## Required neutral probes

For material items, capture the participant's meaning rather than whether they simply say they understood it.

- “In your own words, what was this question asking you?”
- “What did you think about when choosing your answer?”
- “Why did that option fit better than the option next to it?”
- “Was any word or part unclear?”
- “Was the answer you wanted available?”
